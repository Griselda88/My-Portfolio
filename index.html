Beach Cleanup Project Organized by the Rotaract Club of Academic City University

As part of another community service initiative carried out with my fellow executives and members of the Rotaract Club of Academic City University, we organized a beach cleanup project at Labadi Beach in recognition of the month of March, which focused on Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH). The project involved cleaning sections of the beach to promote environmental cleanliness, proper sanitation practices, and public awareness about maintaining cleaner coastal spaces. Through this initiative, we contributed to environmental sustainability while encouraging community responsibility and teamwork.

We facilitated interactive sessions on personal hygiene, financial literacy, peer pressure, and conflict management to equip students with practical life skills beyond the classroom curriculum. To further support their education, we concluded the project by donating stationery and learning materials purchased through team contributions.
